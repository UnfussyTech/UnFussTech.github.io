Our story

We know the problem
from the inside.

Between us, we've spent decades working across enterprise software, product design, and the kind of scrappy small businesses that make up the backbone of the economy. We've seen the same story play out too many times: a small team adopts a tool that promises to change everything, only to find themselves buried in configuration menus, locked out of features they need, and paying more every year for things they don't use.

The best tools we ever worked with weren't the most powerful ones — they were the ones that got out of the way. The ones that let you start work in minutes, not days. The ones your team actually opened every morning without dreading them.

That's what we're building at Unfussy.Tech. Simple, powerful tools that small businesses can pick up instantly — so they can focus on the work that matters, not the software meant to support it.

We believe small businesses should be able to compete on equal footing with the big guys. Not by throwing money at enterprise platforms, but by having access to lean, smart tools that punch above their weight. That's the promise we're building toward, one product at a time.

What we believe

The principles that shape
everything we build.

Every decision we make — what to build, what to cut, how to price it — comes back to these.

🪶
Simplicity is a feature
Complexity is easy. Simplicity takes discipline. We'd rather cut ten things and get one thing exactly right.
⚡
Instant productivity
If your team can't be up and running within minutes, something has gone wrong in the design — not the onboarding.
🤝
Built with real users
We talk to the people using our tools constantly. Their friction is our roadmap. Their wins are our metric.
💰
Honest pricing
One plan, everything included, no surprises. We'll never hold a feature hostage to push you up a tier.
🎯
Less, but better
We ship fewer features than our competitors. That's intentional. Every feature we ship has earned its place.
🌱
In it for the long game
We're building a company around genuine usefulness, not growth-at-any-cost. If it doesn't serve you, it doesn't ship.
