1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

Unfussy.Tech website UnfussyPlanner UnfussyCRM

It covers any personal data collected when you visit our website, sign up for our mailing list, register for beta access, or use any of our products. By using our website or products, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

If you are using our products on behalf of an organisation, the terms agreed between that organisation and Unfussy.Tech may also apply. This policy governs our own practices as a data controller; it does not cover how our customers choose to use or store data within our products, for which they are responsible as data controllers in their own right.

2. Who we are

Unfussy.Tech is operated by Unfussy.Tech Ltd. We build lightweight software tools for small businesses. References to "we", "us", or "our" throughout this policy refer to Unfussy.Tech Ltd.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at privacy@unfussy.tech.

3. Data we collect

Unfussy.Tech website

When you visit our website we may collect:

  • Basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and referring URL, collected automatically by our hosting provider for security and performance purposes
  • Your email address, if you choose to sign up for our newsletter or mailing list
  • Any information you include when contacting us by email

We do not run advertising trackers or analytics scripts that follow you across other websites.

UnfussyPlanner

When you register for and use UnfussyPlanner, we collect:

  • Account information: your name and email address
  • Workspace content: projects, tasks, subtasks, comments, and attachments that you and your team create within the product
  • Usage data: actions taken within the app (for example, tasks created or completed) used to understand how the product is being used and improve it
  • Device and session data: IP address, browser type, and session identifiers for security and support purposes

UnfussyCRM

When you register for and use UnfussyCRM, we collect:

  • Account information: your name and email address
  • CRM content: contact records, company records, deal pipelines, notes, and any other data you choose to store within the product
  • Usage data: actions taken within the app, used to understand product usage and improve the experience
  • Device and session data: IP address, browser type, and session identifiers for security and support purposes
A note on your content: Data you store inside UnfussyPlanner or UnfussyCRM — tasks, contacts, deals, notes — belongs to you. We access it only to deliver the service (for example, to display it back to you) or where required for security, legal compliance, or at your explicit request.

4. How we use your data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Delivering our products and services — providing access to UnfussyPlanner and UnfussyCRM, processing your account, and sending you transactional communications such as account confirmations and password resets
  • Improving our products — analysing aggregated, anonymised usage patterns to understand how people use our tools and where we can make them better
  • Customer support — responding to queries, bug reports, and feedback you send us
  • Security and fraud prevention — detecting and preventing abuse, unauthorised access, and other harmful activity
  • Communications you have opted into — sending product updates and news to subscribers who have signed up to our mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time
  • Legal obligations — complying with applicable laws and responding to lawful requests from authorities

We do not use your data to serve you advertisements, and we do not build advertising profiles based on your usage of our products.

5. Sharing your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — we use a small number of trusted third-party providers to help us operate our services (for example, cloud hosting, email delivery, and payment processing). These providers process data only on our behalf and under our instructions, and are bound by appropriate data processing agreements
  • Other users in your workspace — within UnfussyPlanner and UnfussyCRM, content you create is visible to other members of your workspace, as intended by the collaborative nature of the products
  • Legal requirements — if we are required by law, court order, or regulatory authority to disclose information, we will do so and will notify you where legally permitted
  • Business transfer — if Unfussy.Tech is acquired or merges with another company, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you in advance and you will have the opportunity to delete your account

6. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide you with our services and comply with our legal obligations:

  • Account data is retained for the lifetime of your account, and deleted within 90 days of account closure upon request
  • Workspace content (tasks, contacts, etc.) is deleted within 90 days of account closure
  • Mailing list data is retained until you unsubscribe, after which it is removed within 30 days
  • Server logs and technical data are retained for up to 12 months for security purposes

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at privacy@unfussy.tech.

7. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Our measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS
  • Encryption of data at rest
  • Access controls ensuring only authorised personnel can access personal data
  • Regular review of our security practices

No system is completely secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately at privacy@unfussy.tech.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you are based, you may have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Access — the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — the right to have inaccurate data corrected
  • Deletion — the right to request that we delete your personal data
  • Portability — the right to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — the right to object to certain uses of your data, including direct marketing
  • Restriction — the right to request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Withdrawal of consent — where we rely on your consent to process data (such as for our mailing list), you may withdraw that consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@unfussy.tech. We will respond within 30 days. We will not charge you for making a request unless it is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies necessary to operate our website and products:

  • Session cookies — used to keep you logged in while you use our products. These are deleted when you close your browser
  • Preference cookies — used to remember settings you have chosen, such as display preferences

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking cookies, or any cookies that follow you across other websites. We do not use Google Analytics or similar services.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling session cookies will prevent you from logging in to our products.

10. Children

Our products are intended for use by businesses and their employees, and are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify registered users by email and update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of our website or products after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your data, please get in touch:

  • Email: privacy@unfussy.tech
  • General enquiries: hello@unfussy.com

We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
