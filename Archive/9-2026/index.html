Our honest pitch

We don't have a board reporting module.
And we're proud of it.

The big CRMs are built for companies with a CFO, a sales operations team, and a dedicated admin to keep the thing running. That's not you — not yet. UnfussyCRM is built for right now: the stage where you need to close deals, not configure dashboards.

Our goal is to give you such an unfair advantage that one day you will need those board reporting modules. When that day comes, we'll happily wave you off to a bigger tool. Until then, let's get you there.
