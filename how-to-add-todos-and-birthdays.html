How to add to-dos and birthdays

Keep reminders tied to the right person so nothing slips through the cracks.

Step 1

Open the contact you want to connect to the reminder

At the top, click on Pipeline > Contacts > The name of the person you want associated with the to-do.

This same flow works well for birthdays, follow-ups, and other important reminders that should stay attached to a specific contact.
Step 2

Add the to-do from the right-hand side

On the right, you can see where to add the to-do. Use that area to create a reminder for the contact and keep the task tied to their record.

Adding to-dos

Step 3

View your reminders in Today

If you click “Today” on the left side of the board, you will see all your current and upcoming reminders in one place.
