A different kind of CRM

Most CRMs aren't built
for you.

The big CRM platforms were designed to give managers visibility into sales teams. That's a fine problem to solve — but it's not your problem. You don't have a sales team. You are the sales team. You need a tool that makes you more productive, not one that makes you easier to report on.

UnfussyCRM is a productivity tool. Full stop. It gives you a clear view of your contacts, your pipeline, and exactly what you need to do today — without asking you to become a software administrator to get there.

What traditional CRMs are for
📊 Giving managers pipeline visibility across a team
📋 Generating reports for people who aren't in the deal

The real competition

Your spreadsheet is good.
It just wasn't built for this.

We're not competing with Salesforce or HubSpot. We're competing with your spreadsheet — and we have a lot of respect for it. It's flexible, it's familiar, and it's free. But it can't follow up for you, it can't show you your pipeline at a glance, and it can't tell you that Sarah's been waiting two weeks for a call back.

That's the gap we fill. The simplicity of a spreadsheet with the one thing a spreadsheet can't do: work proactively for you.
